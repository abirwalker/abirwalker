## things I've made so far

I live in Spotify way more than a normal person should, so most of my stuff is Spicetify extensions:

- **[liquid-lyrics](https://github.com/abirwalker/liquid-lyrics)**: Apple Music-style lyrics for Spotify. Word-by-word highlighting, dynamic backgrounds, and more.
- **[onekotify](https://github.com/abirwalker/onekotify)**: the little Oneko cat, but he now lives inside Spotify and chases your cursor. Zero productivity value.
- **[spicy-card](https://github.com/abirwalker/spicy-card)**: lyrics in the sidebar. Line-synced, syllable-synced, or just static. Romanizes Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
